Denys
Honcharenko.

Experience on the ice. A drive to keep improving.

Ukrainian forward Denys Honcharenko has played in Ukraine, the United States, Lithuania, and Italy, and now represents Kyiv Capitals in the Baltic League. In Prague, he found time to focus on the detail he wanted to develop: his skating.

THE HOCKEY JOURNEY

Different leagues.
The same ambition.

A playing career across clubs and countries, with a continued commitment to developing his game.

  1. 2017

    Dnepr Kherson · Ukraine

    Building the foundations.

    His recorded youth career includes Dnepr Kherson at U16, U19, and U20 level.

  2. 2020

    Sokil Kyiv · Ukraine

    Into senior hockey.

    Denys played senior hockey for Sokil Kyiv and represented Ukraine U20 at the 2022 Division I Group B World Junior Championship.

  3. 2022

    Mississippi Sea Wolves / Willmar WarHawks · USA

    A North American chapter.

    The 2022–23 season included Mississippi Sea Wolves in the FPHL and Willmar WarHawks in the NA3HL.

  4. 2023

    Kaunas City · Lithuania

    A new competitive setting.

    With Kaunas City, he competed in the Baltic League, Lithuanian playoffs, and Continental Cup.

  5. 2024

    Pergine · Italy

    Taking his game to Italy.

    In 2024–25, Denys played for Pergine in Italy’s second tier.

  6. 2025

    Kyiv Capitals · Baltic League

    The next club chapter.

    He joined Kyiv Capitals in 2025 and is listed with the club for 2026–27.

WHY HE RETURNS

More than training.
A shared commitment.

THE WORK CONTINUES

Experience matters.
So does the next session.

Denys’s story shows that focused development belongs at every stage of a hockey career. Even after years of senior hockey, there is room to return to the details, sharpen technique, and prepare with purpose.
