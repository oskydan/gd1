THE HOCKEY JOURNEY

New places.
Stronger foundations.

Clubs and countries changed. Summer and winter camps kept the development moving.

  1. 2020

    Patriot · Vinnytsia, Ukraine

    The first steps.

    Yaroslav began his hockey journey with Patriot in Vinnytsia, Ukraine. It was here that he attended his first GD1 camp, starting a connection with Dmitriy Gnitko and a training approach that would stay with him through every move.

  2. 2022

    Streatham · United Kingdom

    A new rink. A new chapter.

    Yaroslav moved to the UK in 2022 because of the war in Ukraine. Joining Streatham meant adapting to a new team and a different training environment. Alongside club hockey, he continued returning to GD1, keeping focused skating work part of his development.

  3. 2023

    Guildford · United Kingdom

    Making every session count.

    During his years at Guildford, limited access to ice made purposeful training especially valuable. Regular summer and winter GD1 camps gave Yaroslav concentrated time to revisit technique, strengthen his skating foundations, and bring that work back to his team. During this period, Yaroslav also earned a first-place finish at the England Junior Ice Hockey Championship U12.

  4. 2025

    Baltic Wolves · Latvia

    A faster pace. The work continues.

    Yaroslav now plays for Baltic Wolves in Latvia, competing at U13 and U14 level. His schedule includes the Latvian Youth Championship, Baltic Youth League, Carpathian Youth League, and other tournaments. Even through a demanding season, he continues attending GD1 camps and developing with guidance from Dmitriy Gnitko.

WHAT TRAVELS WITH HIM

The skating
speaks for itself.

In Latvia, coaches have repeatedly noticed the strength of Yaroslav’s skating, and expressed surprise at how he developed it during three years in the UK, where ice time was limited.

For Yaroslav, GD1 has been a thread of continuity: a place to return to the details, receive guidance, and keep building. The camps complement his club hockey, giving focused attention to the foundations he carries into a busy competitive season.

THE NEXT CHAPTER

Still learning.
Still showing up.

This is not a finished success story. It is the story of a young player finding ways to keep improving, across teams, countries, and seasons.
