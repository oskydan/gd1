THE EXPERIENCE BEHIND THE METHOD

A path built
on the ice.

From professional hockey and national team coaching to development camps across Europe, Dmitriy’s experience shapes GD1’s approach to power skating and individual player development.

  1. Hockey foundations

    Druzhba-78, Kharkiv

    Dmitriy’s hockey journey began at Druzhba-78 in Kharkiv, Ukraine, where he learned the skating fundamentals that would shape his professional career. Through structured practice and repetition, he developed the balance, edge control, and efficient movement that helped him adapt to different teams and styles of play.

    Those lessons remain central to his coaching at GD1: give players a strong technical foundation, explain the purpose behind each movement, and build habits they can rely on in a game.

  2. Playing experience

    Professional hockey & club captaincy

    A forward with playing experience across several European leagues, Dmitriy served as captain of HC Levy in Lviv. His club career brings a practical understanding of competition and team leadership to GD1.

    Ukraine: Donbass Donetsk (2001–02), HC Kharkiv (2009–11), and HC Levy Lviv (2011–13). At Levy, he recorded 32 goals and 53 assists in 71 regular-season games.

    Belarus: His playing record includes HK Vitebsk, Khimik-SKA Novopolotsk, and HK Brest-2.

    Serbia: HK Partizan Beograd (2007–08) and Beostar (2014–15).

    Turkey: Polis Akademisi SK (2009–10).

    Spain: Escor-Bakh Vitoria/Gasteiz (2013–14), where he was part of the Spanish championship-winning team.

    Lithuania: Juodupe (2015–16) and Rokiskis (2016–18).

  3. International coaching

    Experience across Europe

    Dmitriy has coached in Ukraine, Serbia, and Lithuania, and has spent many years working at international and elite player development camps across Europe. His work focuses on power skating, skating mechanics, puck control, and individual player development.

  4. National teams

    Ukraine U16 & Serbia U18

    His coaching experience includes serving on the staff of the Ukraine U16 National Team and as an assistant coach with the Serbia U18 National Team at the World Championship.

  5. GD1 today

    Founder & head coach of GD1

    Dmitriy leads GD1, a player development program specializing in power skating and individual hockey skills. Through focused camps, he applies a methodology used with youth athletes, high-level junior players, and professionals.

THE GD1 PRINCIPLE

“You just need to follow the methodology and our recommendations.”

Dmitriy’s coaching philosophy combines strong skating fundamentals, hockey IQ, and efficient movement patterns that transfer directly into game situations. GD1 camps give players structured time to develop those habits through consistent, purposeful work.
