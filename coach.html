THE EXPERIENCE BEHIND THE METHOD

A path built
on the ice.

Dmitriy’s experience as a player and coach informs GD1’s focus: build strong skating fundamentals through consistent, purposeful work.

  1. 1984 · The roots

    Druzhba-78, Kharkiv

    Druzhba-78 was founded at the Kharkiv Tractor Plant sports school. Dmitriy later became one of its students, learning within a program centered on skating fundamentals and disciplined repetition.

  2. Playing experience

    Fundamentals that travel

    As an adult player, Dmitriy found that his foundation helped him adapt to new teams and take on leadership roles. That experience shaped his belief that sound fundamentals matter at every level.

  3. Coaching

    Passing the method on

    Dmitriy now teaches the skating and power-skating work that formed his own game. Seeing players he has trained make progress has strengthened his commitment to sharing the method and refining it as hockey evolves.

  4. GD1 today

    Development through camps

    Without a permanent rink, GD1 brings players together in focused camps across Europe. Repeated training builds balance, edge control, stride mechanics, acceleration, and endurance over time.

THE GD1 PRINCIPLE

“You just need to follow the methodology and our recommendations.”

Progress takes steady work from both the player and the family. GD1 camps are designed to give that work time to add up.
